02 / Research

Path following and lateral stability of a tractor-semitrailer

Articulated heavy vehicles can sway and lose lateral stability at speed. My research models the tractor-semitrailer as a coupled dynamic system and designs an LQI steering controller that keeps the combination on its path while damping trailer sway.

The work is headed for a research paper, with the option to extend it into a thesis.

Role
Research Assistant, vehicle dynamics lab, UM-Dearborn
Controller
Linear quadratic with integral action (LQI), steering input
Goals
Path tracking error, articulation angle, yaw stability
